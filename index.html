01 · About

A career that keeps changing the question.

I am less interested in collecting titles than in understanding what each place teaches us about institutions, people and public systems.

“The interesting part of administration begins where the policy document ends.”

My professional journey began in technology and moved into the Indian Administrative Service. Since then, I have worked across district administration, urban governance, public administration, water, employment and vocational training.

Each assignment has exposed a different version of the same problem: how do institutions translate intent into outcomes?

This website is an attempt to document that learning — through essays, field observations, research, photographs, travel and conversations. Some pieces will be analytical. Some will be personal. Some may simply be questions I am not yet able to answer.

04 · Journey

A decade in public service.

A concise professional chronology. Detailed stories belong in the writing archive.

2011–12

Tata Elxsi

Software engineering — the beginning of a journey that would eventually move from technology into public administration.

2015

Indian Administrative Service

Joined the IAS, Tamil Nadu cadre, 2015 batch.

2015–21

Field & district administration

Assistant Collector, Sub Collector and Additional District Magistrate responsibilities, including Paramakudi and Thoothukudi.

Urban governance · water · Secretariat

Municipal administration, water-sector responsibilities and senior state-government assignments.

2023–24

District Collector · Ramanathapuram

A particularly formative period in a coastal district shaped by water, livelihoods, agriculture and disaster vulnerability.

2024–25

Employment & Training · Tamil Nadu

Director, Employment and Training — working on ITIs, apprenticeships, industry partnerships and vocational skills.

2025–26

Public policy · Tokyo

Graduate study at GRIPS, Japan, with a growing interest in comparative governance and the India–Japan experience.
